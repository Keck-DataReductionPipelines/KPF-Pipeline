===============
Quality Control
===============

Every keyword registered on the ``QUALITY_CONTROL`` extension, across all four
science levels. Content to follow.

The extension carries two kinds of card: 0/1 quality-check flags, written by
the level's QC class, and diagnostic metrics recorded alongside them. ``Level``
is the data level whose QC pass writes the keyword.
